## National-team memberships

- A player must have only one Player membership per national team. When earlier appearances are documented, extend that membership instead of adding a separate historical stint.
- Preserve the existing active status and known end date when extending a membership. This rule does not apply to club-team stints or separate staff roles.
- For players in the 2024 WAFF U20 Women's reports whose membership starts later, use 2024-11-01 as the membership start date, as requested by the user.
- For Lebanon Under 20 Women player memberships, normalize an existing end date to the last calendar day of that same month. Leave open-ended active memberships open, and preserve start dates and active status.
